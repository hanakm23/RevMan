How to Play

  • Press 1-4 to toggle players on/off
  • Inactive players are controlled by AI
  • Collect pellets for 10 points
  • Collect power pellets for 50 points
  • Chase opposing ghosts when they're blue
  • Avoid other ghosts in normal mode
